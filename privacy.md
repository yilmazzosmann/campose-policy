Privacy Policy for CamPose

Effective Date: November 12, 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how Osman YILMAZ ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information in connection with your use of our mobile application, CamPose (the "App").

1. Information We Collect

We collect information in the following ways:

a) Information You Provide Voluntarily

Account Information: We collect your email address and a username when you create an account to identify you within the App.

User-Generated Content (Photos): The App is a camera app that creates images. These images are stored on your device and are owned by you.

Optionally Shared Content: You have the option to voluntarily share your images with us to be displayed to other users. If you choose to share an image, we will collect that photo and any associated GPS location metadata (if you have also chosen to include it). If you do not press the "share" button, we never collect your photos or location data.

b) Information Collected Automatically

Usage Analytics: We collect metadata about how you use the App, such as the number of images you create and the times you use certain features (like image capture). This data is used in an aggregated and anonymized way to help us understand how people use the App so we can improve it.

Crash Reports: We use Sentry, a third-party service, to automatically collect crash reports and diagnostic information. This helps us identify and fix bugs and improve the App's stability. This data is anonymized to the extent possible and does not include your personal images or exact location.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

To provide and maintain the App's core services (e.g., displaying your optionally shared photos to the community).

To create and manage your account.

To communicate with you, including responding to support requests.

To monitor and analyze crash reports and usage analytics to improve the App's performance and stability.

3. Sharing Your Information & Third-Party Services

We do not sell or "share" your personal information with third parties for their marketing purposes.

However, we use third-party services that are necessary for the App to function:

Supabase: We use Supabase as our backend database to store your account information (email, username) and any User Content (photos, location data) you voluntarily choose to share. You can review their privacy policy here: https://supabase.com/privacy

Sentry: We use Sentry for crash reporting and performance monitoring. You can review their privacy policy here: https://sentry.io/privacy/

4. Your Data Rights & Deletion

All images you create are owned by you. You have the right to request the deletion of your account and any personal data or shared content associated with it that is stored on our servers.

You can request data deletion at any time by contacting us at app.campose@gmail.com. We will process your request and delete your account and associated data within 30 days.

5. Children's Privacy

Our App is not intended for use by anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy within the App and updating the "Effective Date" at the top.
