CamPose - Data Deletion Request

To request the deletion of your CamPose account and all associated data (including your email, username, and any photos you have shared), please send an email to our support address:

app.campose@gmail.com

To help us process your request, please include the following in your email:

Subject Line: "Account Deletion Request"

In the body: The email address and username associated with the CamPose account you want to delete.

We will process your request and confirm the deletion within 30 days.
